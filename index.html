4. Operational Recommendations & Staffing Insights

Peak-Hour Shift & Meal Staggering (9:00 AM – 2:00 PM)

During the primary peak hours, overall consolidated call volume reaches 170 to 203 calls/hour. However, simultaneous agent breaks reduce the available OBS - Phones team down to 12–14 agents, creating a shortage of 3 to 5 agents. Re-staggering lunch breaks into smaller batches across 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM will immediately close this coverage gap.

Cross-Queue Reinforcement (Flex Staffing)

During maximum historical spikes (where volume reaches 300+ calls/hour), OBS - Phones alone cannot sustain service levels. We recommend establishing cross-skilling protocols so that 4 to 5 agents from non-phone teams (such as OBS - Cases or ELD) automatically assist during high-volume spikes.

Evening Capacity Realignment (6:00 PM – 11:00 PM)

After 6:00 PM, call volume plummets to under 28 calls/hour and drops to zero by 7:00 PM. Evening staffing can be scaled down safely, allowing resources to be reallocated toward the busy morning surge (8:00 AM – 12:00 PM).
