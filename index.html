4. Operational Recommendations & Staffing Insights

Peak-Hour Shift & Meal Staggering (9:00 AM – 2:00 PM)

During the main morning surge, consolidated call volume increases significantly, reaching 203 to 253 calls/hour between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM. However, overlapping lunches and breaks reduce the available OBS - Phones team down to just 12–14 agents, generating a coverage deficit of 7 to 11 agents against overall demand. Staggering meal schedules into smaller shifts across 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM is essential to prevent severe queue congestion.

Cross-Queue Reinforcement & Flex Staffing

With historical volume spiking up to 293 calls/hour at 9:00 AM, OBS - Phones (21 agents total roster) cannot handle full consolidated demand alone during peak windows. We recommend triggering automated cross-skilling protocols so that 7 to 11 agents from non-phone or lower-volume teams handle phone overflow during peak windows.
